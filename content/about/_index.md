In a time of multiple and intensifying crises, **Progressive Publics** is rooted
in a commitment to social justice and asserts that both the academy and the
media are public goods that have crucial and entwined roles to play in critical
analysis and knowledge mobilization and dissemination.

This project triangulates three sectors—independent media, the academy, and
the broader public—through connecting crucial questions of the present and
how to live collectively in more just relation to media and scholarly
communication to promote access, social justice, and community engagement.

# Team
## André Goulet

André Goulet is a Community Liason, Outreach Coordinator and Strategic Planner
at Unrigged Media, a collective journalism initiative featuring 31 independent
publishers from across Canada and the Executive Director at the Harbinger
Media Network, Canada's #1 progressive podcast community.


##  Dr. Shama Rangwala

Dr. Shama Rangwala is as Associate Professor at York University whose research
examines the adaptations of liberalism and fascism as represented in cultural
production and the politics of free speech and academic freedom in cultural
production. She is Principal Investigator of _Pedagogy and Praxis_, a project to
develop new forms of public pedagogy that bring scholarly research into dialogue
with broader communities around urgent social and political issues and championing
the university as a public good. Dr. Rangwala is the founding editor of
_Pyriscence_, a digital culture and politics magazine, and a regular
contributor to TV news panels, podcasts, and other media.
